Word data used by RankR's "similar vibe" matching on the Compare screen.

Derived from GloVe vectors (glove.6B, trained on Wikipedia 2014 + Gigaword 5) by Jeffrey Pennington,
Richard Socher and Christopher D. Manning, Stanford NLP, released under the Public Domain Dedication
and License (PDDL). Reduced to the 30,000 most common words and 64 numbers per word by
scripts/build-vibes.py.
